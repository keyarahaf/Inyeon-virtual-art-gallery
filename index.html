About "INYEON" (인연)

인연 (Inyeon) represents the invisible red strings of fate, generational love, and social bonds that bind individuals through the continuous cycle of human existence.

This interactive 3D exhibition follows the bonds connecting people across birth, adulthood, marriage, old age, death and remembrance.

  • 01 Birth (Chulsaeng, Baegil, Dol) Birth was guarded by taboos such as the geumjul, a straw rope hung across the gate to keep bad spirits out, by prayers to Samsin Halmeoni, the grandmother spirit of birth, and by celebrations for surviving the fragile first hundred days (baegil) and the first year (dol).
  • 02 Coming of Age (Gwallye, Gyerye) Historically held between about 15 and 20, with a cap ceremony for young men and a hairpin ceremony for young women. Today adulthood begins legally at 19.
  • 03 Marriage (Honrye) A wedding joined two families, not only two people. The ceremony took place at the bride's home and was followed by pyebaek, the formal bow to the groom's parents.
  • 04 Longevity and Turning Sixty (Hoegap, Suyeonrye) Turning sixty completes the sixty year cycle of the zodiac, once treated as a rebirth and a full circle of time. Today the large celebration often waits until 70 or 80.
  • 05 Funerals and Ancestral Rites (Sangnye, Jesa) Mourning, burial and the yearly jesa rites keep the bond between the living and the dead from breaking.
  • 06 The Whole Life (Pyeongsaengdo) One screen that replays the rooms you have just walked through.
